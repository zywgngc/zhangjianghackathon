==================================================
02. RECREATION OBJECTIVES
==================================================

Recreate the complete core gameplay of
Vampire Survivors using a Chinese Wuxia theme.

The player controls a martial arts hero.

Enemies continuously approach from all directions.

The player moves freely across the battlefield.

All equipped martial arts skills attack automatically.

Defeated enemies drop experience gems.

Collecting experience increases the player's level.

Every level-up presents three upgrade choices.

The player may acquire new martial arts,
upgrade existing martial arts, or learn
passive internal cultivation techniques.

Specific combinations of martial arts
and passive techniques unlock ultimate skills.

The player must survive increasingly difficult
enemy waves and defeat the final Boss.

Preserve the following systems:

1. Free movement
2. Automatic attacks
3. Continuous enemy spawning
4. Experience collection
5. Character leveling
6. Three-choice skill upgrades
7. Multiple simultaneous weapons
8. Passive attribute upgrades
9. Skill evolution
10. Elite enemies
11. Boss battles
12. Level progression
13. Gold rewards
14. Permanent character upgrades
15. Local save data

SPECIAL GAMEPLAY REQUIREMENT:

This game uses an advertisement-style
accelerated progression system.

The player must experience noticeable
power growth during the first minute.

The game must support large-scale
enemy elimination and visually impressive
martial arts combinations.

Progression targets:

0–15 seconds:
Basic automatic combat and first kills.

15–30 seconds:
Access to at least two active skills.

30–60 seconds:
A normal player should be able to obtain
three or four active skills.

60–120 seconds:
Noticeable increases in damage,
projectile count, and attack coverage.

120–180 seconds:
At least one valid progression route
must allow the acquisition of an ultimate skill.

180 seconds onward:
Large-scale enemy waves and powerful
multi-skill combinations.

Do not require lengthy grinding before
the player can experience powerful skills.

VISUAL MODIFICATION:

Replace traditional Western fantasy
weapons and monsters with Chinese Wuxia
martial arts, sword techniques, palm strikes,
hidden weapons, internal cultivation,
and ancient martial arts environments.

The gameplay should retain the recognizable
survivors-style combat loop.

Use original or appropriately licensed assets
rather than copying protected artwork,
characters, music, or branding.

06.10 Fast Progression System

At 5 seconds:

At 15 seconds:

Grant another free upgrade selection.

Free upgrade selections do not consume XP.

Free upgrade selections do not increase
the character's numerical level.

If the player still owns only one active skill
at 15 seconds, at least one upgrade choice
must be a new active skill.

For the first 3 minutes:

Increase the selection weight
of existing skill upgrades.

At 90 seconds:

Prioritize upgrades related to
the player's closest achievable evolution.

When evolution conditions are satisfied:

The corresponding ultimate skill must appear
in the next upgrade selection.

The game must contain at least one
valid progression route that allows
ultimate evolution within 180 seconds.

Do not automatically give every player
an ultimate skill.

Players must still satisfy the
appropriate evolution requirements.

==================================================

08.1 Automatic Combat

All active martial arts attack automatically.

Each skill has an independent cooldown.

Multiple skills can attack simultaneously.

Skills must use actual damage calculations.

Visual effects must correspond to
real attack events.

08.2 Enemy AI

Normal enemies:

Locate the player.

Move toward the player.

Attack through contact or ranged attacks.

Enemies may use simple local separation
to avoid complete overlap.

Do not spawn normal enemies directly
on top of the player.

08.3 Enemy Spawn Positions

Spawn enemies outside the visible camera area.

Recommended spawn distance:

At least 80 pixels beyond
the visible screen boundary.

Enemies must move into the battlefield
after spawning.

08.4 Contact Damage

When an enemy collides with the player:

- Apply damage.
- Trigger a hit animation.
- Activate invulnerability.
- Prevent repeated collision damage
  during invulnerability.

Player invulnerability duration:
0.6 seconds.

08.5 Player Death

If player HP <= 0:

- Stop movement.
- Stop combat.
- Stop enemy spawning.
- Stop timers.
- Trigger death animation.
- Display defeat screen.

08.6 Enemy Death

When enemy HP <= 0:

- Stop enemy behavior.
- Trigger death effect.
- Drop experience.
- Roll additional drop probabilities.
- Remove enemy from active gameplay.
- Return pooled objects when appropriate.
