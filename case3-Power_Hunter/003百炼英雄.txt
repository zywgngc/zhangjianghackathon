02. RECREATION OBJECTIVES
==================================================

Recreate the core numerical power comparison
gameplay commonly shown in Hero Wars advertisements.

The player controls one hero.

Every hero and enemy has a visible
numerical combat power value.

The player can defeat an enemy only when
the player's current power is strictly
greater than the enemy's power.

After defeating an enemy, the player's power
increases by the defeated enemy's entire power.

Weapons are distributed around the level.

Collecting a weapon increases player power
by a fixed numerical amount.

The player must determine a suitable sequence
of enemy defeats and weapon pickups.

The final objective is to become powerful enough
to defeat the level Boss.

Preserve the following gameplay systems:

1. Numerical combat power
2. Visible power labels above characters
3. Power comparison
4. Click-to-select interaction
5. Automatic movement
6. Automatic attack animation
7. Enemy defeat
8. Enemy power absorption
9. Weapon collection
10. Weapon power bonuses
11. Progressive enemy selection
12. Boss battle
13. Level completion
14. Level progression
15. Restart and replay

GAMEPLAY STRUCTURE:

Use a freely navigable open field.

Enemies and weapons are distributed
across the map.

The player can click any reachable
enemy or collectible.

The hero automatically travels
toward the selected target.

Do not use fixed tower floors.

Do not use continuous automatic attacking.

Do not use automatic enemy selection.

Do not use a virtual joystick as
the primary control method.

The player must actively choose
which enemy or weapon to interact with.

VISUAL REQUIREMENT:

Use a stylized 3D fantasy adventure theme.

The main character is a fantasy hero.

Enemies include monsters, skeletons,
armored enemies, and other fantasy creatures.

Use original or appropriately licensed
assets instead of copying protected
artwork from the reference game.

==================================================
04. PLAYER CONTROL SYSTEM
==================================================

04.1 Primary Interaction

The primary interaction is tapping
or clicking objects.

Supported targets:

- Enemy
- Weapon
- Empty ground
- Boss
- UI controls

04.2 Click Enemy

When the player selects an enemy:

Read the current player power P.

Read the selected enemy power E.

If P > E:

Set the selected enemy as the active target.

Move the hero automatically toward the enemy.

Stop within attack range.

Play the attack animation.

Apply the power absorption result.

Remove the defeated enemy.

If P <= E:

Do not initiate movement toward the enemy.

Display:

战力不足

Highlight the enemy power label in red.

Allow the player to choose another target.

04.3 Click Weapon

When the player clicks a weapon:

Set the weapon as the current target.

Find a valid path to the weapon.

Move automatically toward it.

Stop within pickup range.

Play the pickup animation.

Increase player power.

Remove the collected weapon.

04.4 Click Ground

Clicking an empty reachable location
moves the hero to that location.

Ground movement does not trigger combat.

The hero must not automatically attack
nearby enemies.

04.5 Movement Parameters

World coordinate system:

X = horizontal ground direction
Y = vertical height
Z = ground depth

Player movement speed:
5 world units per second

Player collision radius:
0.4 world units

Enemy interaction range:
1.2 world units

Weapon pickup range:
0.8 world units

Movement must be frame-rate independent.

04.6 Automatic Pathfinding

Use a navigation grid or equivalent
pathfinding system.

The hero must navigate around
static obstacles.

The hero must not pass through
walls or impassable terrain.

If a target cannot be reached:

Cancel movement.

Display:

无法到达目标

04.7 Target Changes

If the player clicks a different target
while moving:

Cancel the previous movement target.

Calculate a new path.

Move toward the newly selected target.

Do not teleport.

04.8 Interaction Lock

During the final attack animation
and power calculation:

Temporarily prevent conflicting interactions.

After the interaction completes,
restore normal input.

04.9 Desktop Controls

Mouse clicking must perform
the same actions as mobile tapping.

04.10 Camera

Use an elevated isometric-style
third-person camera.

The camera should follow the player
with smooth movement.

The player must remain clearly visible.

04.11 Pause

When paused:

- Player movement stops
- Target interactions stop
- Animations pause
- Game timers pause
- Combat resolution stops
- UI remains interactive

==================================================
06. CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
==================================================

06.1 Combat Power

Let:

P = player combat power

E = enemy combat power

Both must be positive integers.

06.2 Enemy Defeat Condition

The player can defeat an enemy only if:

P > E

Strict inequality is required.

If:

P = E

The player cannot defeat the enemy.

If:

P < E

The player cannot defeat the enemy.

06.3 Successful Combat

After a successful enemy defeat:

P_new = P + E

Example:

Player power:
10

Enemy power:
6

Result:

10 + 6 = 16

Player power becomes:
16

06.4 Invalid Combat

When:

P <= E

The attack must not execute.

No power is lost.

No enemy is removed.

The player remains alive.

Show:

战力不足

06.5 Weapon Collection

Let:

W = weapon bonus

After collecting a weapon:

P_new = P + W

Example:

Player power:
16

Weapon:
精钢剑 +20

Result:

16 + 20 = 36

06.6 Weapon Rules

Every weapon is collectible once.

After collection:

- Apply the bonus
- Play the pickup animation
- Update the power label
- Remove the weapon
- Record the item as collected

The same weapon cannot be collected twice.

06.7 Power Growth Feedback

After a successful kill:

Display:

+E

Animate the player power label
from its previous value to the new value.

After collecting a weapon:

Display:

+W

Play a distinct weapon pickup effect.

06.8 Attack Animation

A successful combat interaction must include:

1. Hero approaches target.
2. Hero stops in attack range.
3. Hero faces the target.
4. Hero plays a short melee attack.
5. Target plays a hit animation.
6. Target is defeated.
7. Combat power increases.

Default attack animation duration:
0.6 seconds.

Power absorption animation duration:
0.5 seconds.

06.9 Enemy Defeat

After a successful attack:

- Enemy becomes inactive.
- Enemy plays defeat animation.
- Enemy disappears.
- Enemy cannot be selected again.
- Player receives enemy power.

06.10 Boss Defeat

Bosses use the same comparison rule.

If P > BossPower:

The Boss can be defeated.

After defeating the Boss:

- Trigger Boss defeat animation
- Stop gameplay movement
- Display victory effects
- Open victory screen

06.11 Boss Selection

If player power is insufficient:

Display:

战力不足，继续提升战力

The player must collect weapons
or defeat weaker enemies first.

06.12 Selection Highlight

When the player selects a valid target:

Show a selection indicator.

When a target is defeated or collected:

Remove the indicator.

06.13 Numerical Display

Display all combat power values
as integers.

Do not display decimals.

Do not use abbreviations such as K
for values below 10000.

Maximum supported combat power:
999999

06.14 Power Comparison Integrity

All gameplay systems must use
the same authoritative combat power value.

Do not use separate hidden HP,
attack, or defense values
to determine combat success.

==================================================
08. COMBAT AND COLLISION SYSTEM
==================================================

08.1 Combat Trigger

Combat begins only through
an intentional enemy selection.

08.2 Combat Validation

Validate combat power both:

- When the enemy is selected
- Immediately before resolving the attack

This prevents stale target state.

08.3 Attack Range

The hero must stop within
1.2 world units of the enemy.

08.4 Attack Direction

Before attacking, rotate the hero
toward the target.

08.5 Attack Resolution

If player power is greater than enemy power:

Play attack animation.

Play enemy defeat animation.

Increase player power.

Remove enemy.

08.6 Invalid Combat

If player power is insufficient:

Do not initiate an attack.

Show warning feedback.

08.7 Collision

The hero cannot pass through:

Walls
Large rocks
Impassable structures

Enemies must be treated as
obstacles during pathfinding.

The selected enemy can be approached
to a valid interaction point.

08.8 Weapon Pickup

Weapons are collected when the hero
reaches the configured pickup radius.

Pickup range:
0.8 world units.

08.9 Interaction Cancellation

Changing the active target cancels
the previous movement action.

Interactions already in the
final resolution phase must complete
before accepting another target.

08.10 Enemy Removal

Defeated enemies must be removed
from collision and selection systems.

08.11 Target Selection

Use Babylon.js scene picking
or an equivalent picking mechanism.

Touch and mouse selection must
identify the correct game object.

08.12 No Automatic Enemy Aggression

Enemies do not attack the player
unless a future version explicitly
introduces such behavior.

08.13 Failure Handling

An invalid enemy selection does not
immediately cause game over.

If the level reaches a state where:

- The Boss cannot be defeated
- No weaker enemy remains
- No uncollected weapon can improve power

Display:

当前无法继续提升战力

Offer:

重新开始
返回主页

Do not silently leave the player
in an unsolvable level state.

==================================================

The first release uses numerical combat
power as its primary progression system.

Do not introduce a separate currency
or complex equipment economy.

10.2 Combat Power

Combat power is temporary
and resets at the start of each level.

Weapons provide power bonuses
for the current level only.

between levels.

Use localStorage.

- Highest unlocked level
- Completed levels
- Audio preferences

10.6 Restart

Restarting the current level must reset:

- Player combat power
- Defeated enemies
- Collected weapons
- Enemy positions
- Weapon positions
- Boss state
- Current movement target

10.7 Replay

Previously completed levels
may be replayed.

Replaying does not remove
previously unlocked levels.

10.8 No Permanent Combat Upgrades

Do not implement permanent power upgrades
in the initial release.

The level puzzle must retain
its intended fixed starting power.

Do not require registration,
login, or remote storage.
