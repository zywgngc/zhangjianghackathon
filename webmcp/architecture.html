Material portal boundary

Our MAS calls an application port. A client adapter connects to the teammate-owned MCP portal and validates its responses.

Proposed I/O port

MaterialPortalPort.search(request, { signal }) → Promise<MaterialSearchResult>

DirectionPayloadResponsibility
InputPurpose, description, image references, architecture constraints, requested material types, candidate limitOur Researcher formulates the request; our adapter maps it to the portal.
OutputMultiple candidates, source references, available license evidence, provenance, limitationsThe teammate’s portal returns materials; our Reviewer evaluates them.
FailureStructured error, retryability, optional retry delayOur orchestrator records gaps and applies bounded retries.

Version and request IDs are checked at the boundary. Defaults are visible and editable. Unknown source metadata remains unknown. Fixture results never qualify as real research.
